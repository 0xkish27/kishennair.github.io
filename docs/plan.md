# Arcanum: business plan

## The idea

An escape room the size of a city. Couples (and pairs of friends) play a self-guided evening adventure through real KL landmarks on their phones: riddles, locks, mini games, collectible cards and a story ending. Inspired by Heal Code Malaysia's immersive mall games, but outdoors, made for two, and needing no staff on site.

## Positioning

- **Who:** couples on dates first, friend pairs second. Corporate team packages later.
- **Against Quest KL:** Quest KL runs facilitated team-building hunts. Arcanum is the self-guided date night, built around the couple (know-me locks, split clues across both phones, dares, a shared scrapbook).

## Partner-free model

- Every stop is a free public place: River of Life, Dataran Merdeka, KLCC Park and its free Lake Symphony shows, Saloma Link, Kwai Chai Hong, Jalan Alor, Bukit Bintang.
- Every route starts and ends at an LRT or MRT station. The GOKL city bus is free for Malaysians.
- Spending is optional and capped inside the game (for example, the RM 40 purse on The Feast of Riddles). Players choose any stall, so no deals are needed.
- The reward is the game itself: cards, the final word, the story ending and the scrapbook. Café or brand perks can be added later, once foot traffic can be shown.

## Pricing to test

A full night out stays under RM 100 for two, including transport and food.

## Gamification

- **Challenges:** location riddles, GPS check-in, riddles, code locks, split clues, know-me locks built from the couple's answers, dares, photo tasks, purse tasks, blind orders, a sound hunt, a hum duel and a time-capsule letter.
- **Mini games, one set per journey:** light the lanterns and carry the beam (Lantern Path); catch the falling stars and trace the gate (Starfall); a pairs memory game and the spirit's scale (Feast); catch the beat and the echo lock (Echo).
- **Collection:** 47 cards in total. 19 common, 19 rare (play a stop with no hints or mistakes), 4 legendary (play a whole journey clean) and 5 secret.
- **Season leaderboard:** best run per journey plus card points. Each journey counts once per season.
- **The Keys:** the top three couples each season get a metal NFC card.
  - Obsidian (1st): lifetime pass, access to The Midnight Door (a secret seasonal journey ending at a real hidden box), and they write a riddle for the next season.
  - Silver (2nd): a year of free journeys plus one Midnight Door.
  - Bronze (3rd): six free journeys plus early access to new ones.

## Launch phases

1. **Prove it:** one journey on the web app, 30 to 50 test couples.
2. **Launch:** all four journeys, real payments, TikTok and Instagram using real couples' recap videos.
3. **Grow:** membership, the season leaderboard, Klook and hotel listings, festive chapters.
4. **Scale:** native apps, the Sealed Box, Penang and Melaka editions.

## Tech

Progressive web app first; native apps later. Supabase for auth, data and photo storage. A Malaysian payment gateway (Billplz, ToyyibPay or iPay88). Photos compressed to about 0.5 MB each, so 15,000 photos is roughly 7.5 GB. PDPA-compliant privacy, consent and account deletion.
