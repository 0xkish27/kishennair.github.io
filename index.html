tonight, and only tonight

The night changes. So do the rules.

Every evening brings a new omen that bends how the journeys play, and a new riddle anyone can answer from here. Answer it on consecutive nights to build your streak and climb the season standings.

the journeys

Four doors into the dark. One of them is already calling you.

Every journey runs through free public places, so the only thing you pay for is the adventure. Each one has its own story, its own riddles and its own deck of cards to earn.

how the night works

You play it. The city hides it.

Follow the riddle

Each clue points to a real place. Work out where, or ask for the map if you are stuck.

Check in together

Your phone confirms you are there and the stop's challenges unseal.

Break the lock

Riddles, code locks, split clues and questions only your partner can answer.

Earn the card

Every stop gives you a card and a letter. Collect them all to speak the final word.

the decks · pick one

Three styles of magic.

These are three directions for the cards players earn. Each journey gets its own exclusive set that can only be won by playing. Turn a card, then pick a style. The prototype will use it.

the collection

Collect every card. Some are rarer than others.

Cards carry across every journey you play. Play a stop clean, with no hints and no wrong answers, and its card turns Rare. Play a whole journey clean and you earn its Legendary. A few secret cards only appear for couples who do something unusual.

season i · the veil

The top three couples win a Key.

Every journey you finish, every card you collect and every Rare you earn adds to your season score. Each journey counts once per season, so the best run wins, not the most tickets. When the season closes on the night of the full moon, the top three couples receive a physical Key in the post.

Scoring: your best points on each journey, plus 25 per card, 75 per Rare and 300 per Legendary.

the keys

A real metal card. It opens real doors.

Three Keys are made each season and never reprinted. Each one has an NFC chip, so a tap on any phone opens the winning couple's scrapbook. Move your cursor over them.

the purse

The magic is free. The city is the board.

  • Every stop is a free public place. Parks, bridges, laneways and heritage streets. Nothing to book, nobody to partner with.
  • Getting there is cheap. Each journey starts and ends at an LRT or MRT station, and the GOKL city bus is free for Malaysians.
  • Spending is optional and capped. Food tasks set a limit, and the in-game purse tracks every ringgit.
  • The reward is the game itself. Cards, the final word, a story ending and a scrapbook of your night.
